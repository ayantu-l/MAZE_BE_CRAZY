SURVIVAL PROTOCOLS

🔥 OVERHEAT

Your ammo is unlimited, but fire too fast and the weapon jams for 5 seconds. Let it cool down or become monster food.

🧬 MUTATION

Killed monsters drop orbs. Collect them to upgrade your gun. If a monster nearby absorbs one, it evolves and gets stronger.

📡 ECHO

The maze is dark. Press E to reveal it for a few seconds. Warning: every monster learns your exact position.
